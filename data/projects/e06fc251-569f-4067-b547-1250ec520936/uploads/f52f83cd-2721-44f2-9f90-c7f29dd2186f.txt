
Business Requirements Document

Section 1: Overview
This system shall process uploaded documents and extract requirements.
All processing must complete within 30 seconds per document.

Section 2: Functional Requirements
The system shall support PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, MD, and TXT file formats.
Each uploaded document shall be chunked and embedded into a vector store.
Users shall be able to search documents using semantic similarity queries.

Section 3: Non-Functional Requirements
The system must maintain 99.9% uptime.
All data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Response times for API endpoints must be under 500ms for 95th percentile.
